# Milestone 4 Submission Checklist

## Purpose

This guide lists exactly what must be prepared before submitting the Final Milestone PoA. It is a production checklist, not evidence by itself.

## A. Evidence folders

Prepare the following public files or URLs:

```text
Milestone 4/
├── README.md                    # Catalyst evidence table and final links
├── final-report-draft.md        # Working report; rename only when finalized
├── collaboration/
│   ├── meeting-summary.md       # Project, participants, challenge, support, outcome, timestamps
│   └── follow-up-evidence.*     # Screenshot/PDF/link proving acknowledgement or next step
├── engagement/
│   ├── post-1-dev.png
│   ├── post-1-dailydev.png
│   ├── post-2-dev.png
│   ├── post-2-dailydev.png
│   └── discussion-replies.png
└── media/
    └── final-closeout-video.mp4
```

Do not upload private account credentials, private meeting links, access tokens, personal contact information, or unredacted participant data.

## B. Engagement screenshots

For each post, capture a screenshot that visibly includes:

1. post title and URL/platform;
2. author identity;
3. publication date;
4. reactions/upvotes;
5. comment count and representative comments;
6. capture date in the file caption or accompanying Markdown.

Record counts as “observed on `<YYYY-MM-DD>`” because platform statistics can change.

## C. External collaboration meeting

### Before recording

- Obtain participants’ permission to publish the recording.
- State the external project’s name and public URL.
- Use an agenda that produces assessable evidence:
  1. project introduction;
  2. challenge explained by the external team;
  3. HTLABS analysis and proposed assistance;
  4. concrete solution, recommendation, example or next step;
  5. partner acknowledgement and agreed outcome.

### During the meeting

Aim for 15–30 minutes. Ensure names/roles and both projects are stated verbally or shown on screen. Demonstrate the relevant issue, architecture, code or device workflow when possible.

Write down three timestamps while reviewing the recording:

- challenge;
- HTLABS assistance/solution;
- outcome/acknowledgement.

### After the meeting

Publish:

- a public or unlisted-accessible video link usable by Catalyst reviewers;
- a written summary containing participants, date, project links and timestamps;
- one follow-up artefact: issue comment, PR, technical note, shared solution or partner acknowledgement.

A video showing only HTLABS presenting its own project is not enough. It must show assistance to a separate IoT or blockchain project.

## D. Final close-out report

Update `final-report-draft.md` with:

1. project purpose and five delivered templates;
2. achievement summary for Milestones 1–3;
3. two public posts and dated engagement metrics;
4. representative community questions and HTLABS responses;
5. external collaboration project, challenge, assistance and outcome;
6. technical and delivery challenges;
7. measurable impact and reusable public assets;
8. links to the final video and all evidence.

Remove words such as “successful”, “completed” or “approved” unless the linked evidence directly supports them.

## E. Final close-out video

Recommended format:

| Item | Requirement |
|---|---|
| Duration | 2–4 minutes |
| Resolution | 1920×1080, 16:9 |
| Language | English voice-over or clear English captions |
| Opening | Project name, Project ID 1300008, final-milestone title |
| Achievements | Show the five templates with real demo footage or screenshots |
| Community | Show both posts and representative discussions |
| Collaboration | Show the external project, challenge, HTLABS assistance and outcome |
| Challenges | Briefly state technical/delivery challenges and resolutions |
| Impact | Explain who can reuse the templates and where to find them |
| Closing | Show public repository, final-report URL and Catalyst project page |

Use actual repository screens, issue/PR pages, demo footage and meeting excerpts. Do not use a text-only slide video as the final evidence.

## F. Pre-submission validation

- Open every URL in a logged-out/incognito browser.
- Confirm the meeting video and final video do not require permission requests.
- Confirm screenshots are readable at normal zoom.
- Verify all meeting timestamps.
- Ensure the final report and video describe the same evidence and outcomes.
- Search the repository for `MISSING`, `TODO`, placeholder `#` links and private information.
- Submit only after all checks pass.
